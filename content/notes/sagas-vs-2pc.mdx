Seedling — mostly questions so far.

**2PC** gives atomicity across resources at the cost of a coordinator that can block everyone when it disappears. Fine inside one database cluster, painful across services you don't own.

**Sagas** split the work into local transactions, each with a *compensating* action. You trade atomicity for availability and accept that intermediate states are visible.

## Choreography or orchestration?

- Choreography: services react to each other's events. Loose coupling, hard to see the whole flow.
- Orchestration: one component drives the steps. Easy to reason about, one more thing to keep alive.

My bias for payment flows is orchestration, because "where is this transaction right now?" is a question support asks every day.
